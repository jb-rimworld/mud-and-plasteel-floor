# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-24)
- Built and tested on RimWorld 1.7; checked in game with and without any DLC, in English and German.
- Beauty and Cleanliness sliders now update the room readouts at once, not only the floors.
- The Beauty slider moves in whole steps, because vanilla rounds the Beauty stat to a whole number.
- Settings window: sliders no longer overlap their captions.
- All settings texts and all floor names and descriptions are translated (English, German); the Vacstone floor text only loads with Odyssey.
- Plasteel floors now have a description.
